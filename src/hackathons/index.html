The experience

A deadline changes how you think.

Hackathons compress the development process. You have to decide what matters, what can wait, and what you can actually build.

01

Understand

Understand the challenge before rushing into implementation. Map the problem first.

02

Decide

Decide what to build, what to prioritise, and how to approach the architecture.

03

Build

Turn your reasoning into a working software solution before the clock runs out.

04

Present

Explain what you built, why you built it, and demonstrate how it works to the panel.

After the event

You leave with more than a score.

Every challenge is an opportunity to produce something tangible and learn how you perform when there isn't a step-by-step tutorial guiding your hands.
