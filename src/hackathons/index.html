The experience

A deadline changes how you think.

Hackathons compress the development process. You have to decide what matters, what can wait, and what you can actually build.

01

Understand

Understand the challenge before rushing into implementation.

02

Decide

Decide what to build, what to prioritise and how to approach it.

03

Build

Turn your reasoning into a working solution before the clock runs out.

04

Present

Explain what you built, why you built it and how it works.
